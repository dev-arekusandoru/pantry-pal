# Ideas (not planned yet)

Brainstorm notes. Nothing here is built. Move an item to `BACKLOG.md` when we commit to it.

## Gamification direction

- Tone: cozy, Animal Crossing vibes, soft rewards. No guilt, nothing breaks or punishes.
- Players: designed for one person, a couple, or a family.
- Biggest stress today: doing the prep and cooking.

## Ideas

- **Stove streak**: each home-cooked meal lights a burner, and four lit burners make a "Full stove" week. A pilot light carries over from week to week; a missed week only dims it. A kettle whistles at milestones.
- **Meal draft**: Raku deals 3 recipe cards for each open night, and you swipe. Cards are weighted by pantry, expiring items, favorites, effort, and one wild card. Couple mode: each person swipes, and a recipe you both keep is a match.
- **Traveling vendor**: a weekly visitor brings about 5 new recipes from outside the app. Sources: TheMealDB (free API), Spoonacular (paid), URL import (in the backlog). Avoid scraping sites in bulk.
- **Prep cards**: split prep into small cards that fill a mise-en-place tray. Prep party: people claim cards from a shared tray, with a soft timer and Raku cheering.
- **Living Raku**: in `BACKLOG.md`.
